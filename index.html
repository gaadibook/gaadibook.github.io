FAQ

Questions, answered

How does the free trial work?
Every new workshop gets 30 days with all features, free. You don't need to give any payment details to start. Before the trial ends, the app shows the plan for your workshop size and you can choose to pay.
How do I pay, and which payment methods work?
You pay inside the app through Google Play. You can use any method your Google Play account supports, such as UPI, cards or Google Play balance. Google Play sends the receipt to your email.
How do I cancel? Can I get a refund?
Cancel anytime in the Google Play Store app under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. There is no cancellation fee, and your plan keeps working until the end of the month you have paid for. For wrong or double charges, write to us within 7 days for a full refund. Read the full refund and cancellation policy.
How is the plan chosen for my workshop?
By the number of people in your workshop, counting the owner and all staff. Up to 5 people is Small, 6 to 10 is Standard, 11 to 30 is Large and 31 to 100 is Extra Large.
Does it need internet?
Yes, to save. Every job card and bill is saved online so all phones in your workshop see the same records. A copy stays on your phone so you can look up old records quickly, even when the network is slow.
What happens to my records if I stop paying?
Your saved job cards, bills and other records are not deleted when a plan ends. You can also ask us to delete your account and data; the steps are in the privacy policy.
Which phones does it work on?
GaadiBook is an Android app, available on Google Play. Owners and staff each use their own phone and sign in with their own Google account.
