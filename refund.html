GaadiBook — Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This policy applies to paid GaadiBook plans bought in the GaadiBook Android app. All payments are processed by Google Play. Please also read our Terms of Service.

1. Free trial

Every new workshop gets a 30-day free trial with all features. Nothing is charged during the trial, and no payment details are needed to start it.

2. Plans and billing

  • Plans are monthly: Small ₹299, Standard ₹399, Large ₹599, Extra Large ₹999 a month (all taxes included).
  • Payment is taken by Google Play when you buy, and again on the same date every month until you cancel.
  • Google Play sends a receipt to the email address of your Google account.

3. How to cancel

  • Open the Google Play Store app, tap your profile picture, then Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • Choose GaadiBook and tap Cancel subscription.
  • You can cancel anytime. No cancellation fee is charged.
  • After cancelling, your plan keeps working until the end of the month you have already paid for. It is not renewed after that.
  • Deleting the app does not cancel the plan. Please cancel in Google Play.

4. Refunds

  • If you were charged by mistake, charged twice, or the paid plan did not start in the app, write to us within 7 days of the payment at gaadibook.team@gmail.com with the Google Play order number (it starts with GPA.). We will check and refund the full amount for that payment.
  • You can also ask Google directly within 48 hours of a payment from Google Play: Google Play refund help.
  • Refunds go back to the same payment method used in Google Play. Google usually completes them within 5 to 10 working days, depending on your bank or UPI app.
  • Apart from the cases above, a month already paid for is not refunded for the unused days, but you can cancel so you are not charged again.

5. Your records

Your saved job cards, bills and other records are not deleted when a plan is cancelled, refunded or ends.

6. Contact

For billing questions, cancellation help or refund requests: contact.sitalabs@gmail.com or gaadibook.team@gmail.com. GaadiBook is run by Seethalekshmi A (SITALABS), Vellooparampil, Karuvatta PO, Alappuzha, Kerala 690517, India. We reply within 2 working days.
